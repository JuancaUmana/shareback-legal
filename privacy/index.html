Shareback

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Shareback helps you send people the photos they're in. It's built so your photos, contacts and face data stay on your iPhone. Shareback has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics.

Photos

  • You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, so Shareback can only see the photos you choose.
  • Shareback looks for faces in them on your iPhone. It never uploads your photos.
  • A photo leaves your iPhone only when you send it: in Messages, in another app you pick from the share sheet (such as WhatsApp, Instagram or Mail), or by pasting photos you copied. Once it's in another app, that app's privacy policy applies.
  • Shareback saves a copy of each photo you choose on your iPhone, so your event is still there if Shareback closes. The copies are deleted when you tap Done after sending, or when you start a new event.

Contacts

You choose people with Apple's contact picker, so Shareback only sees the contact you pick: their name and the phone number or email you choose. It uses them to fill in the message for that person. For someone who isn't in your contacts, you can type a name instead.

Face data

To group photos by person, Shareback turns each face into a face signature: a list of numbers made by a face recognition model. A signature isn't a photo, but it can identify a person, so many laws treat it as biometric data.

  • During an event, signatures are saved on your iPhone with the event's photos, so the event is still there if Shareback closes. They're deleted when you tap Done after sending, or when you start a new event. They're left out of iCloud and computer backups.
  • If you turn on Remember People and give your consent, Shareback saves the signatures of the people you name, with their name and the phone number or email you picked, if any. It uses them only to ask "Is this …?" at your next event.
  • Saved signatures stay on your iPhone. They're never uploaded, shared, sold or used for advertising, and they're left out of iCloud and computer backups.
  • Only name people who agree to be remembered. Don't name children without permission from a parent or guardian.

How long face data is kept

  • A remembered person's signatures are deleted when you tap Forget or Forget Everyone, or when you delete Shareback.
  • A person you haven't named or confirmed in 12 months is forgotten automatically.
  • Shareback keeps at most the 30 most recent signatures for each person.

Your choices

You can turn Remember People on or off, and forget anyone, at any time from Remembered People on Shareback's start screen. Because everything is stored only on your iPhone, we can't see it, access it or delete it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new effective date. If a change affects face data, Shareback will ask for your consent again.
