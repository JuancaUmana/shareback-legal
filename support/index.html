Shareback finds the people in an event's photos and helps you send each person only the photos they're in. Everything happens on your iPhone. There's no account to make.

How it works

  • Tap Choose Photos and pick the photos from an event: a trip, a party, a dinner.
  • Shareback groups the faces it finds. Tap a face to say who it is: pick a contact, type a name, or say it's you.
  • Open a person to check their photos. Tap the small hexagon on a photo to leave it out.
  • Tap Send. For each person, choose Messages, another app like WhatsApp or Instagram, or Copy to paste the photos anywhere. Nothing is sent until you send it there.

Shareback didn't find someone

Faces need to be reasonably large and facing the camera. Faces that are very small, turned away, blurry or covered may not be found. Faces seen in only one photo are listed under "faces seen only once."

Two different people were grouped together, or one person was split in two

Open the person, tap Change, and pick the right name. Naming two groups with the same contact puts their photos together.

Does Shareback upload my photos?

No. Faces are found and matched on your iPhone. Shareback has no servers. A photo leaves your iPhone only when you send it yourself.

How do I send photos to someone who isn't in my contacts?

Tap their face, choose Type a Name, then send their photos with another app or copy them.

What does Remember People do?

If you turn it on, Shareback keeps a face signature, a set of numbers and not a photo, for each person you name, so it can ask "Is this Maria?" at your next event. It stays on your iPhone. You can forget anyone, or turn it off, in Remembered People.

How do I delete everything?

Tap Done after sending to delete the event's photos and faces from Shareback. Forget everyone in Remembered People, or delete the app, to remove the rest.
